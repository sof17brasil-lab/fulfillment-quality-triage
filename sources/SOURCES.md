# Sources

Every outside fact used in this project, where it comes from, and how well it has been checked.

**How to use this file**

1. Open each link yourself.
2. Find the exact words in the "Exact words" column.
3. Tick the box in "You checked".
4. Save a copy of each PDF or page into `sources/originals/` (links break, and you want proof of what the page said on the day you used it).

A fact goes on the website only when its box is ticked.

**Status meanings**

- **Confirmed**: I re-read the original on 11 Oct 2026 and matched the wording word for word.
- **Read once**: I read it through a summary only. The gist is reliable, the exact wording is not. Re-check before quoting.
- **Not confirmed**: I could not verify it. Do not use until you have.
